# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

VoiceRooms provides voice conversations by invitation, including invitations created in Telegram inline mode.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/Vite and LiveKit support voice, screen sharing, room chat, encrypted temporary files, room-specific names and owner moderation. Preserve existing admission, encryption and Telegram behavior. Camera calling is outside scope.

## Brand Commitments

The owner chose a calm, familiar interface like Telegram calls. Preserve the VoiceRooms name. Local redesign and preview in an isolated worktree are authorized.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md and frontend/src. A development-only illustrative call allows inspection without a media server. Label it as a preview and never report a real connection.

## Product Principles

Participants first. Keep call controls visible. Distinguish private and public protection. Concise Russian labels, accessible focus and phone layouts.

## Primary usage

The owner specifies 80% phone users and 20% desktop users. Phone convenience is the primary design criterion. Validate thumb reach, touch targets, short viewport states and chat input before desktop refinements.
